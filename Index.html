About Valley Homestay

Welcome to Valley Homestay, located in the remote and beautiful village of Chasak Bhatori. Nestled in the heart of the Pangi Valley, our homestay offers a peaceful escape into nature's lap. Enjoy breathtaking views of rivers, grounds, and snow-covered mountains right from your window. At night, marvel at the clear skies offering unparalleled stargazing opportunities and a stunning view of the Milky Way.

Accommodations

We offer comfortable and cozy lodging with three operational bedrooms designed for a restful stay. The shared bathroom facilities are maintained with utmost cleanliness to ensure your comfort.
